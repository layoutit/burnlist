## implement
Implement the assigned item completely, run focused checks, and leave the
workspace ready for the declared repository validation.

Do not edit Burnlist lifecycle files or complete the item directly. Burnlist
owns transitions and completion.

## review
Independently review the validated candidate against the item contract. Approve
when it is correct and proportionate. Treat P0 safety, security, data-loss, or
authority findings as blockers that require escalation; reject P1 high-impact
defects and P2 defects that materially affect acceptance. P3 improvements are
non-blocking follow-ups. P4 notes are optional and should not add noise.
